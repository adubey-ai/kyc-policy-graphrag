# PEP screening SOP (branch one-pager)

When a politically exposed person asks to change of address, enhanced due diligence is mandatory even if onboarding EDD already happened.

High risk pin codes on list HRC-22 also require EDD. Freeze authority: Financial Crime Unit can freeze CBS posting for 72 hours. One of the two checkers must come from the Financial Crime Unit.

Current account opening: businesses still need a GST certificate in addition to OVD.
