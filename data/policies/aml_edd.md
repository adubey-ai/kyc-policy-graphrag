# AML and Enhanced Due Diligence

Policy ID: POL-AML-007. Owner: Financial Crime Unit. Regulator: PMLA and RBI KYC.

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) applies to PEP customers, high-risk pin codes in list HRC-22, and cash-intensive current accounts above INR 50 lakh monthly turnover.

EDD requires form SOF-Q1 (source of funds), a 12-month account-behaviour review, and dual Checker sign-off. One of the two Checkers must be from the Financial Crime Unit, not the originating branch.

The Financial Crime Unit can freeze CBS posting for 72 hours while EDD is pending. During a freeze, neither Maker nor branch Checker can write KYC or address changes.

False-match alerts on PEP screening must be closed with reason code PEP-FM-3 and a second reviewer. Unresolved PEP alerts block Checker approval.
