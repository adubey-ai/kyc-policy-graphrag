# Q3 Operations Circular: Field-extraction bots

Internal circular, not a policy ID restatement. Effective immediately.

Any field-extraction bot used at the branch, including the IASW packet, is classified as Maker staff for control purposes. It is prohibited from updating core banking customer masters. A human Checker remains the only role that may post.

If the first Officially Valid Document check fails, do not retry locally; the case must go to the Central KYC Desk.
