# KYC Onboarding Policy (Savings and Current)

Policy ID: POL-KYC-001. Owner: Retail Compliance. Regulator: RBI Master Direction on KYC.

A new savings account requires Officially Valid Document (OVD) for identity and a second OVD or utility bill for proof of address. Acceptable identity OVDs are Passport, Aadhaar, Voter ID, and Driving Licence.

A new current account additionally requires a GST certificate when the customer is a registered business, and a cancelled cheque of the existing operating account.

The Maker role in the branch can capture KYC fields and attach documents. The Maker cannot post the account to the Core Banking System (CBS). Only the Checker role can approve the KYC packet and trigger the CBS write.

If the customer is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is mandatory before Checker approval. EDD includes source-of-funds questionnaire SOF-Q1 and dual Checker sign-off.

Failed OVD verification on the first attempt must be routed to the Central KYC Desk, not re-attempted by the same Maker.
